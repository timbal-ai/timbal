## Retirement and pricing notes

The deprecation ledger removes GLM-5, GLM-4.7, Gemma 3n E4B, Cogito v2.1, and Mistral Small 24B from serverless with no on-demand endpoint support. Gemma 3 27B was also retired from dedicated inference. These IDs are no longer in this catalog. Monthly reserved arrangements, where offered, require a separate agreement. GPT-OSS 20B and Kimi K2.7 Code remain listed above for dedicated deployments only.

Qwen 3.8 cached input has conflicting official rates: &#36;0.50 in the serverless catalog versus &#36;0.25 on pricing and model pages. Its cached price is deliberately unassigned. MiniMax M2.7 and Qwen2.5 7B have conflicting availability listings; their existing dedicated restriction is retained pending confirmation.
