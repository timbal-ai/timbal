## Serving modes and retirement

All token prices above are Standard mode. Priority, Fast, US-only routing, batch, reserved throughput, and dedicated deployments have different rates. See the [current mode rate card](https://docs.fireworks.ai/serverless/pricing); this catalog does not automatically price nonstandard modes. The September 25 retirement applies to serverless DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731, Kimi K2.6, Kimi K2.7 Code, and GLM-5.2. Their dedicated deployments remain available.
